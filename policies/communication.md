# Communication and writing

Scope: explanations, issues, comments, documents, commit messages, PRs and UI copy.
Follow explicit user requests and the destination's language, terminology, templates
and required detail. Communicate in the user's language; write repository content
in English unless the task or destination requires another language.

- Lead with the answer, outcome or requested action. Give each sentence one main point.
- Use consistent terms and explicit actors and actions. Do not invent an unknown actor.
- Distinguish verified facts, inferences and proposals; state what remains unknown.
- Preserve conditions, negation, quantities, units, exceptions and uncertainty.
  Remove filler without removing necessary reasoning or examples.
- Prefer direct verbs, active voice and familiar words. Prioritize accuracy and
  natural grammar over rigid sentence lengths or controlled vocabulary.
- Preserve code, commands, identifiers and quotations. Translate meaning and
  certainty faithfully; retain established bilingual terminology.
- Adapt structure and detail to the reader and task. Do not force headings or lists
  into short replies. Explicit stylistic requests can override these defaults.

For Korean, reduce stacked nouns, long modifiers, unnecessary passive forms and
translation-like phrasing; use natural particles and sentence endings. For English,
avoid unnecessary nominalizations, idioms and ambiguous pronouns; keep articles and
prepositions that make technical relationships clear.

Use [issue-writing](../skills/issue-writing/SKILL.md) for issue bodies and
[PR workflow](pr-workflow.md) for reviewable PR delivery. Make procedures actionable
and describe the user's next action in UI copy. Completion claims follow
[evidence](evidence.md).
